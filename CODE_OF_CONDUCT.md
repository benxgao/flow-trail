# Code of Conduct

## The short version

- Be decent.
- Assume the other person is smart and busy.
- Critique the work, not the worker.

## What is expected

- **Disagree with reasons.**
  - "This rule cost me two hours because the baseline was optional" is useful.
  - "This is bad" is not.
- **Stay on the substance.**
  - The subject is a workflow for recording decisions.
  - Politics, nationality, gender, religion and editor choice are off topic.
- **Accept a "no" without a fight.**
  - Refusals are deliberate and documented in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, anywhere the project is discussed.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions, flooding threads, bad faith.

## Scope

- Issues, pull requests, commit messages.
- Any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

- Report to **hi@benxgao.com**.
- Include what happened and where. The maintainer reads reports only.

Responses, by severity:

1. A private word.
2. A public correction.
3. A temporary ban.
4. A permanent ban.

- You are told which applies and why.
- A maintainer who is the subject of a report does not handle it.
